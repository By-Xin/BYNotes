## Concept Map

- Asymptotic convergence: law of large numbers and delta method.
- Information and sufficiency: Fisher information, sufficient statistics, and factorization.
- UMVU theory: Rao-Blackwell improvement, Lehmann-Scheffé uniqueness, and Cramér-Rao lower bounds.
- Rank and sign based tests: sign test, Wilcoxon tests, Mann-Whitney U, and Kruskal-Wallis.
