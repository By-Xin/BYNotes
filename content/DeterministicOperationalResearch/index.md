## Concept Map

- Convex sets, affine geometry, topology, and existence of optimal solutions.
- Projections, normal and tangent cones, and proximal operators.
- Separation, supporting hyperplanes, support functions, and Farkas lemma.
- Polyhedra, basic feasible solutions, degeneracy, extreme points, and extreme rays.
