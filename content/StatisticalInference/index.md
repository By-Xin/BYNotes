## Concept Map

- Asymptotic convergence: law of large numbers and delta method.
- Information and sufficiency: Fisher information, sufficient statistics, and factorization.
- Estimation theory: UMVU theory, Rao-Blackwell improvement, Lehmann-Scheffe uniqueness, and Cramer-Rao lower bounds.
- Nonparametric testing: sign test, Wilcoxon tests, Mann-Whitney U, and Kruskal-Wallis.
