## 1. Test Families

Nonparametric statistics tests and their applications:

- [Sign Test](./Sign-Test.md)
  - Single population quantile or median test.
  - Single population proportion test.
  - Two paired population median test.
- [Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test](./Wilcoxon-Signed-Rank-Test.md)
  - Single population quantile or median test.
  - Two paired population median test.
- [Wilcoxon Rank Sum Test](./Wilcoxon-Rank-Sum-Test.md)
  - Two independent population median test.
- [Mann-Whitney U Test](./Mann-Whitney-U-Test.md)
  - Equivalent to Wilcoxon Rank Sum Test.
  - Two independent population median test.
- [Kruskal-Wallis Test](./Kruskal-Wallis-Test.md)
  - Multiple independent population median test.
  - Nonparametric ANOVA.
- Friedman Test
  - Multiple related population median test.
  - Nonparametric repeated-measures ANOVA.
- Runs Test
  - Randomness test for a sequence of data.
