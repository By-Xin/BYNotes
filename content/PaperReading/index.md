## Note Shape

Each paper note should keep the useful parts close to the top:

1. Citation and reading status.
2. One-sentence contribution.
3. Problem setup and notation.
4. Method and key technical device.
5. Experiments or evidence.
6. Open questions, limitations, and follow-up readings.
